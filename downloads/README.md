# JARVIS + Orb download

[JARVIS_3_5_1_and_Orb.zip](JARVIS_3_5_1_and_Orb.zip): extract it, then double-click
`INSTALL_EVERYTHING.bat`. It updates JARVIS (keeping your settings and notes), installs
the JARVIS voice Orb (FCC, orb only) and downloads its offline voice and ears. After that,
everything runs on your PC. This folder is a download drop, not part of FCC.
