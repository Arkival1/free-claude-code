How our loaf is made

The steps happen in this order every day. The bars show how long each one takes against the whole 31 hours.

9:00 pm · day 1

Mix

Stoneground flour, water at 78% hydration, levain and sea salt.

9:30 pm · 4 h

Bulk

Four sets of folds in the first two hours build the strength.

1:30 am · 30 min

Shape

Pre-shape, rest, then a tight boule into a linen-lined basket.

2:00 am · 24.5 h

Cold proof

A full day at 3 °C for the sour, the blisters and the open crumb.

3:40 am · day 3

Bake

Scored by hand, 20 min with steam at 250 °C, 22 min to a dark crust.
